Residential Painting in Lucknow

Home Painting Services Overview

Whether preparing a newly purchased flat, refreshing an occupied family residence, or protecting exterior walls against Lucknow's severe weather, our services are delivered with disciplined surface preparation and clear written scopes.

Specialty 01

Interior Wall & Ceiling Painting

Interior painting requires careful attention to wall smoothness, clean cutting-in along ceilings and door trims, and strict cleanliness inside your living space. We handle everything from basic repainting fresheners to comprehensive two-coat putty leveling for newly handed-over flats.

  • Living rooms, master bedrooms, children's rooms, kitchens & dining spaces
  • Smooth putty touch-ups, hairline crack repair, and acrylic wall primer
  • Matte, satin, and washable luxury emulsion finishes

Specialty 02

Exterior House Painting

Exterior surfaces in Lucknow endure 45°C summer heat, intense UV rays, and heavy monsoons. Without proper surface scraping and a high-grade exterior primer, standard paint flakes within two seasons. We thoroughly wash walls, treat fungal growth, seal structural plaster cracks, and apply weather-guard elastomeric exterior systems.

  • Independent bungalows, kothis, boundary walls, and parapets
  • Algae & black mold neutralization with specialized masonry cleaners
  • Crack-bridging exterior primer followed by anti-dust weather coatings

Anti-Algae & UV Weather Guard
Specialty 03

Repainting Occupied Homes

Repainting a home while your family is living in it can be stressful if painters are untidy. We specialize in lived-in residential repainting: we systematically mask one zone at a time, tape all electronics, use low-odor emulsions, and complete tidy daily cleanup so your family stays comfortable.

  • Complete furniture and floor protection with heavy-duty drop sheets
  • Addressing old nail holes, minor scuffs, switchboard marks, and smoke stains
  • Quick turnover options for tenants, landlords, and pre-festival refreshes
