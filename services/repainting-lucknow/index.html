House Repainting Services in Lucknow

Repainting an occupied home without turning your everyday life into chaos. We shift furniture, mask floors, patch nail holes, and complete room-by-room repainting smoothly.

Designed for Busy Lived-in Homes

The biggest hesitation homeowners have when considering a repaint is the mess: paint droplets on marble floors, paint dust settled inside wardrobes, and having nowhere to sleep.

Lucknow PaintCare solves this with structured room-by-room repainting:

  • Zoned Execution: We work in one or two rooms at a time, keeping the rest of your house accessible and functional.
  • Total Dust Masking: Beds, sofas, TVs, and dining sets are clustered and tightly wrapped in clean plastic dust sheeting.
  • Floor Drop Cloths: Heavy cotton canvas drop cloths line transit hallways and room floors to protect against roller splatter.
  • End-of-Day Cleanup: At 6 PM daily, ladders are gathered, drop cloths folded, and floors swept so your family enjoys an orderly evening.

Understanding Your Repaint Scope: Refresh vs. Restoration

Not every home requires the same amount of preparation. Here is how we categorize repainting projects in Lucknow:

Scope Aspect Option A: Quick Colour Refresh Option B: Substantial Surface Repair
Wall Condition Sound existing paint, minor nail holes, faint scuff marks, no flaking. Cracking plaster, peeling putty, dampness stains, multiple old paint layers.
Putty Treatment Spot touch-up putty on nail holes and minor scuffs only. Extensive scraping followed by full 1 or 2 coats of acrylic wall putty.
Primer Requirement Spot primer over filled areas (or overall if changing dark to light colour). Complete coat of penetrating primer across all stripped surfaces.
Typical Timeline (2-3 BHK) 3 to 5 working days. 7 to 10 working days (due to required putty drying intervals).
Best Suited For Rental turnovers, pre-Diwali refreshes, selling a property. Homes repainted after 5-8 years, or homes with previous dampness damage.

A Note on Changing Wall Colours

If your existing rooms feature dark red, royal blue, or deep golden yellow accent walls and you wish to transition to a calm off-white or ivory, a standard two-coat application may bleed undertones. In our quotation, we specify an opaque white transition primer coat to ensure your new shade appears clean and true to the swatch.

House Repainting FAQs

Can our family stay in the house while you repaint?
Yes. The vast majority of our repainting clients in Lucknow remain in their homes throughout the project. Because we work room-by-room and use low-odor water-based emulsions, your daily routine remains manageable.
How do you treat nail holes and old picture hooks?
We remove old plastic rawl plugs, clean out loose drywall powder, pack the cavity with non-shrinking filler, smooth with fine putty, sand flush with the wall plane, and spot-prime prior to top coats.
What if one of our walls has dampness near the skirting?
Dampness must never be painted over directly. During our site visit, we identify the origin (e.g. bathroom plumbing seepage or ground capillary moisture). We recommend treating the root dampness and applying a specialized waterproofing barrier coat before repainting.
