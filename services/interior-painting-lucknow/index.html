Interior Painting Services in Lucknow

From smooth plaster preparation to flawless top-coat finish, our interior painting service delivers long-lasting elegance with meticulous protection for floors, doors, and furniture.

Who this service is for & what it covers

Whether you are moving into a newly handed-over 2 BHK or 3 BHK flat in Gomti Nagar Extension or repainting an established bungalow in Aliganj, interior painting defines how your home feels every single day.

We cover the full spectrum of interior spaces:

  • Living & Dining Rooms: Focal accent walls, clean borders along cornice moldings, and durable, wipeable sheens.
  • Bedrooms: Soothing, low-odor matte or satin finishes with dust-controlled application.
  • Ceilings: Crisp, ultra-flat ceiling whites that eliminate roller lap marks and diffuse light softly.
  • Doors, Grills & Trims: High-adhesion enamel or PU coating on interior metal grills and wooden door frames.

Why Surface Preparation Matters Most

A high-end emulsion cannot compensate for uneven putty or chalking plaster. At Lucknow PaintCare, we assess wall substrate condition prior to quoting and apply the correct sequence:

1. Inspection & Scraping

Loose paint, old flaky patches, and grease are scraped down to solid base plaster using steel putty knives.

2. Deep Primer Coat

Water-thinnable penetrating acrylic primer seals the porosity of the bare wall so putty adheres firmly.

3. Acrylic Putty Smoothing

Applied in 2 cross-hatched thin coats. Once cured, walls are sanded with fine 220-grit paper under angled inspection light.

4. Two Finish Coats

Two uniform coats of premium emulsion rolled with micro-fiber sleeves, yielding a smooth, stipple-free finish.

Choosing the Right Interior Finish

We help homeowners select sheens that match family lifestyle and maintenance requirements:

Low Reflection

Velvet Matte Finish

Absorbs light softly and conceals minor surface plaster imperfections. Ideal for living rooms and bedrooms where an understated, elegant ambiance is preferred.

Balanced Sheen

Satin / Silk Finish

Soft, subtle lustre with moderate light reflection. Offers good washability, making it suitable for corridors, dining areas, and kids' rooms where accidental handprints occur.

High Durability

Washable Luxury Emulsion

Teflon or cross-linked polymer emulsions designed for stain resistance and frequent wet-sponge cleaning. Recommended for busy family households.

What Determines the Cost of Interior Painting?

The actual investment depends on real site variables rather than an arbitrary flat rate:

  • Wall Condition: Lived-in homes with clean walls require simple sanding & 2 coats, whereas walls with peeling putty require complete scraping and leveling.
  • Ceiling Height & Detail: Standard 10-foot ceilings vs double-height stairwells requiring scaffolding.
  • Ceiling Inclusions: Adding POP false ceilings with coves and multiple cut-outs requires dedicated masking and manual brushing.
  • Occupancy & Furniture: Empty flats allow faster rolling; fully furnished flats require progressive masking and shifting.

Interior Painting FAQs

Does your quotation include ceiling painting?
Yes, our written quotation clearly states whether ceilings are included. We typically recommend painting ceilings concurrently with walls so your entire room reflects consistent freshness and brightness.
How do you minimize paint odor inside the home?
We use high-grade water-thinned interior acrylic emulsions with low VOC content. Unlike old solvent-based distempers, modern water-based emulsions dry quickly and have minimal lingering scent, making them safe for families and children.
Will you help shift beds, sofas, and heavy furniture?
Yes. Our team assists in shifting furniture away from perimeter walls to the center of each room, where everything is neatly enveloped under heavy-gauge protective plastic sheets before painting commences.
