2. Information Collection and the WhatsApp Handoff

Our website is designed as a direct lead-generation platform. In version 1.0:

  • No Web Server Storage: Our estimate forms do not submit your personal information into a web server database or cloud backend. Instead, the form validates your required inputs (Name, Phone, Locality, Service) and formats a structured text message.
  • WhatsApp Handoff: When you click the continue button, your prefilled text message is passed to the WhatsApp platform via a secure deep link (wa.me). You can review and edit all text before sending.
  • Third-Party Terms: Once the handoff to WhatsApp occurs, WhatsApp's own privacy terms and end-to-end encryption govern your chat communication.

3. How We Use Your Project Details

Any information you communicate with us—whether by phone, WhatsApp, or during an in-person site visit—is used exclusively to:

  • Understand your painting requirements and coordinate site measurements.
  • Prepare and deliver an itemized quotation for your home.
  • Schedule painting crews and execute agreed painting work.

We never sell, lease, rent, or trade your contact details to third-party telemarketers or external subcontractor marketplaces.

4. Analytics & Technical Privacy

To understand how visitors navigate our website and improve user experience, we may utilize standard web analytics (such as Google Analytics 4):

  • Strict PII Safeguard: Our website code explicitly blocks any personal data (such as your name, mobile number, full message, or exact street address) from being sent into analytics tools.
  • Anonymous Interaction Events: Only general category events (such as button clicks or chosen service type like "Interior Painting") may be anonymously recorded.
  • IP Anonymization: IP anonymization is enforced where analytics is enabled.
