Consumer Advice

What a Transparent Painting Quote Must State

Before hiring any painter in Lucknow, insist on a clear scope. A vague single-line rate often leads to disputes over whether ceiling painting, putty scraping, or furniture moving were included.

What We Always Specify in Writing

  • Surface preparation scope: whether walls need sanding only, touch-up putty, or 2 full coats of acrylic putty.
  • Primer & coat count: explicitly stating 1 primer coat + 2 finish emulsion coats.
  • Ceiling painting inclusion: clearly stating whether ceilings and balconies are included or separate.
  • Protection & cleanup: floor drop sheets, switchboard masking, and daily tidiness.
  • Realistic schedule: estimated start date and working days needed for completion.

Customer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, truthful answers to common questions about house painting in Lucknow.

Do you paint both occupied homes and newly handed-over flats?
Yes. For occupied homes, we carefully shift and cover furniture with plastic drop cloths and complete one or two rooms at a time to minimize disruption. For new flats or bare plaster, we perform complete surface preparation, including sanding, primer, and two coats of acrylic putty before applying finish paint.
Is a site inspection necessary before you provide a final quotation?
Yes. While we can provide rough ballparks if you share room counts, BHK size, or photos on WhatsApp, an accurate quotation requires seeing the wall condition. Minor hair-line cracks require simple filler, whereas dampness, efflorescence (shora), or loose plaster need specific remedies before painting.
Do I need to buy the paint myself, or do you provide labour and materials?
We offer both options. Most homeowners prefer our comprehensive material + labour service, where we supply genuine, factory-sealed paint buckets, primers, putties, rollers, and masking tapes. If you already have your own paint, we are also happy to provide skilled labour-only service.
How do you protect floors, fans, and switchboards?
Before a single can of paint is opened, our team tapes all electrical switch plates and door hinges, uses drop cloths across the flooring, and protects ceiling fans and light fixtures. At the end of every working day, the crew sweeps up paint dust and packs tools neatly.
How long does it typically take to paint an apartment or house?
A standard 2 BHK or 3 BHK interior repainting refresh usually takes 4 to 6 working days depending on surface condition. Fresh painting requiring two full coats of putty, drying intervals, and fine sanding typically requires 7 to 10 days. We provide a firm timeline commitment in your written quote.
