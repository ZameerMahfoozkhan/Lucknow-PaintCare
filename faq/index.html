Frequently Asked Customer Questions

Direct, honest answers about painting scope, pricing drivers, furniture masking, surface preparation, and timelines for Lucknow homeowners.

What types of home painting services do you provide in Lucknow?
We specialize in residential painting: interior wall and ceiling painting, exterior house weather-coating, repainting lived-in apartments and bungalows, rental turnover refreshes, and surface preparation (sanding, acrylic primer, and putty leveling).
How do I request an estimate for my home?
You can submit our simple Estimate Request Form or message us on WhatsApp with your locality in Lucknow, property type (e.g. 2 BHK, 3 BHK, bungalow), and rooms needing paint. We will provide an initial consultation and schedule a free in-person site inspection to measure wall area and inspect surface condition.
What factors affect house painting cost in Lucknow?
Cost depends on actual paintable wall area, whether ceilings are included, the existing wall condition (sound paint vs flaking plaster or dampness), the depth of surface preparation required (light sanding vs two coats of acrylic putty), the paint grade selected (economy vs luxury washable emulsion), and whether the property is empty or fully furnished. Read our complete Painting Cost Guide for full details.
Do you paint both newly handed-over flats and older lived-in homes?
Yes. For newly handed-over flats with bare plaster, we execute full surface preparation: stone-sanding, primer application, 2 full coats of acrylic wall putty, 220-grit fine sanding, and two top coats of emulsion. For older lived-in homes, we focus on furniture protection, patching nail holes, stain blocking, and low-dust execution.
Is wall preparation and putty included in the quotation?
Yes. Our written quotation explicitly specifies the preparation scope: whether walls receive spot-putty touch-ups for nail holes, or full double-coat acrylic putty leveling. We never hide preparation costs as an unexpected extra on your final bill.
Do I need to move furniture before your painters arrive?
You do not need to move heavy furniture yourself. We ask only that you clear small personal items, electronics, and fragile valuables. Our crew shifts beds, sofas, and wardrobes to the room center and wraps them under thick, clean plastic dust covers.
Can I share photos on WhatsApp before scheduling a site visit?
Absolutely. Sharing brief room photos or a 30-second video of your walls on WhatsApp helps us understand the scope immediately. We can identify peeling areas, ceiling heights, and room layout before our estimator arrives.
How long does an average painting job take?
A standard 2 BHK or 3 BHK interior repainting refresh typically takes 4 to 6 working days. Complete fresh painting requiring two coats of putty with mandatory drying and sanding intervals takes 7 to 10 working days. Exterior painting for a two-story home typically takes 8 to 12 days depending on weather.
Which paint brand and finish can I choose?
We work with top Indian manufacturer paint systems (including Asian Paints, Berger, Nerolac, and Dulux) in original, factory-sealed containers. We help you choose the right finish—matte, silk/satin, or high-scrub washable luxury emulsion—based on room lighting, family usage, and maintenance preferences.
Which areas of Lucknow do you serve?
We serve all residential localities across Lucknow, including Gomti Nagar, Gomti Nagar Extension, Indira Nagar, Aliganj, Hazratganj, Mahanagar, Jankipuram, Ashiyana, Vikas Nagar, Rajajipuram, and surrounding residential societies.
